Since we're done with the competition season, I figured I should do some sort of reflection here.

Wednesday, May 6th

This season has been my favorite yet. I have seen the people that I am closest to go so far and I've learned so much about robots and leadership and people in general.

At the start of this year, the subteam leads and co-captains made large sticky notes of goals for the season. Unfortunately I don't currently have pictures of all of them, but if I end up finding those I will type out the goals we set here. At the end of the competition season, our lead mentor had all of the leads come in and reflect on how we met our goals. I do have a picture of this, and I'll type out what I said.

```
Name: Kona

Position: Co-captain

1. Did I meet my goals?
Generally, yes, I think a lot of the goals were met, but some of them (ex. Google Calendar) were not properly communicated. Some also just didn't happen.

2. What did I learn about leadership and myself?
This team's leadership is more chaotic than I expected. I also do still struggle with public speaking, but less.

3. What do I want my successor to know that I didn't?
People listen to you & follow you. If you go somewhere, the team will most likely follow. But you also need to learn how people interact with each other, like specific groups of people.
```

As I said in the reflection there, I think many of the goals were met. I also think we didn't set enough goals that really stretched the team, because we didn't really know what kind of goals we were setting. I thought they were supposed to be "things that should happen" and not "dreams for the team," if that makes sense.

I'm gonna talk about how I think the season actually went NOT in regard to those goals now.

This season went better than I could have imagined. Sure, we didn't make it to the world championships, but I never really thought that would happen anyway. Recently, I talked to a previous co-captain about the season, and he said that we shouldn't have qualified for districts because of how our team was managed, which is an interesting opinion. While I agree that our team has issues with organization and leadership, I don't think that it means we didn't deserve to go to DCMP. If we had qualified by winning matches and ranking well (the normal way), I would probably agree with this sentiment, however since we qualified because of the Impact award, I don't agree with him. We qualified for the district championship due to our contributions to our community, not due to how our team and robot performed. I also think that the way we performed in competitions is not representative of "how well the season went." I think that this season has been the best (at least in the 4 years I've been on the team) in terms of preparation. In my previous years, I don't think enough teaching had been done directly to members, which led to a void of knowledge and experience for 2025's season, some of which carried forward to 2026. I think that, during this year, the student leadership (as well as other experienced team members) have been putting in a lot more effort than in previous years to retain knowledge for future years. I say this a lot ot the other student leads, but we're preparing the team to do incredible next year. Also, the new members are interacting with each other far more than I have seen before, other than *maybe* during my freshman year. I think that the bonds between all of them will be strong and they will work as a team more closely than before.

## Leadership

I'm not supposed to comment on this while I'm on the team (specifically for co-captains, which will be decided on the 12th. Sub-team leads are up to current and future co-captains as well as the mentors, so I will be part of that decision). I cannot directly share my opinions or try to influence votes, and neither can any other members that won't be here for next season. I simply want to say that I think that my opinions are similar to the opinions that other team members have shared with me, and I am confident that the members that are allowed to vote will choose whoever they think will best lead them next year.

## Final Statements and Goals

Everyone on the team is integral to its functionality.  Over the offseason, members who will return next year need to learn as much as physically possible, and graduating members need to teach as much as physically possible.

Once the co-captains for next year are picked, I will help teach our successors as much as I can about this strange position that I have been in for a year.
